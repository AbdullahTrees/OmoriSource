# Historical device snapshots

These are exact files previously kept in `omori-handover/device-pmjs`, moved here on
2026-10-29. They record past device installations; they are not automatically current and
must not silently overwrite the maintained `launcher/` patches or release templates.

Maintain new host helpers under `tools/device/`, OMORI patches under `launcher/`, and release
packaging in `portmaster-builder/omori-pmjs`. Keep an exact installed snapshot when recording
a device experiment, and describe which version it represents in its evidence report.
